Harmony Lab v0.9.18 — Independent Progressions Key

Based on v0.9.17 Midnight Jazz. Progressions now has its own key mode and root, offering 12 Major + 12 Minor keys without changing Circle. Changing Circle no longer resets Progressions. All progression labels, chord voicings, and playback follow the independent progression key. Selection change cancels ongoing progression playback and clears a stale selected progression. The Midnight Jazz design and Android safe areas are unchanged.

Web: deploy the separate Netlify ZIP with index.html at its root.
Android: build from this full project in Android Studio.
